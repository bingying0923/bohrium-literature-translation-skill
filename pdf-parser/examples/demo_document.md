## 1 Introduction（第 2 页）

Lead contamination of drinking water remains a public health problem in many regions. Portable sensors may enable screening where laboratory facilities are unavailable.

## 2 Methods（第 2 页）

We collected 36 water samples from three cities between March and June 2024. Samples were filtered and passed through a chelating membrane. The calibration curve was fitted with a linear model, and the limit of detection was calculated as three times the standard deviation of blank measurements.
