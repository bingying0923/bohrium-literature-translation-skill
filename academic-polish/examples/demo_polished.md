## 段落 1

Detecting lead ions rapidly in drinking water remains a practical challenge. We demonstrate a low-cost and easy to use colorimetric sensor based on a chelating membrane. The data were collected from 36 water samples in three cities. The limit of detection is 0.8 µg/L [3], which is lower than values reported previously (Fig. 2). Samples did not require pretreatment, and the assay can be completed in 12 min. These results may suggest that the method could be applied in resource-limited settings, although field validation is still needed.
