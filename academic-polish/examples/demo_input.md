Rapid detection of lead ions in drinking water remains a practical challenge. We demonstrate a cheap and easy-to-use colorimetric sensor that is based on a chelating membrane. The data was collected from 36 water samples in three cities. The detection limit is 0.8 µg/L [3], which is lower than previous reports (Fig. 2). Samples did not require pretreatment, and the assay can be finished in 12 min. This may suggest that the method could be applied in resource-limited settings, although field validation is still needed.
