3 Results

The sensor detected lead ions in all 36 samples within 12 min. Recovery ranged from 92% to 108% across the three tested matrices. The limit of detection of 0.8 µg/L was lower than that of the colorimetric reference method.

4 Discussion

These results may suggest that the method could be applied in resource-limited settings. A limitation is that field validation in humid conditions has not yet been performed.
